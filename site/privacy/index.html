POLICY / 2026-08-28

Privacy.

Scrubbed Log Casefile is designed to keep incident data on your device.

CLI data

The CLI makes no network requests and has no telemetry. It reads only the files you specify and writes the encrypted archive you request. Passwords stay in your environment and are not stored by the product.

Browser demo

Demo text is processed in your browser and is not transmitted or saved. The offline service worker caches only public site assets.

Licenses

If you buy or verify a team-pack license, the browser contacts Sociobot at api.sociobot.in. The token and a verification timestamp are stored in localStorage under keys beginning sb_license:scrubbed-log-casefile. You can remove them by clearing this site's storage. Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout as merchant of record under its own checkout privacy notice.

Analytics and contact

This site ships no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. Questions can be filed in the public source repository.
